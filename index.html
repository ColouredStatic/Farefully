Plans around people

Love it, Not keen and Never suggest preferences actually change what appears. Different households can have separate profiles on the same device.

Uses what you have

Add pantry ingredients and Farefully deducts them from the shopping basket before calculating what you need to buy.

💷

Keeps an eye on spend

Your budget influences the recommendations. Supermarket models and recent verified product snapshots help make the estimate more useful.

🛡️

Hard exclusions stay hard

Never suggest is a genuine block. Wheat-free, gluten-free and Coeliac are treated as distinct requirements rather than one vague filter.

❄️

Plans for busy weeks

Batch-cook and freezer-friendly meals are surfaced deliberately, alongside quicker dinners and familiar family staples.
