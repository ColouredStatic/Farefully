Practical meal planning

Make dietary requirements part of the plan.

A meal plan should start with the foods your household can eat. Farefully helps narrow recipe choices using saved preferences and exclusions, rather than asking you to inspect every recipe from scratch.

Start planning freeCompare Free and Plus

Tell the planner what matters

Set the dietary pattern and extra exclusions for your household. Farefully distinguishes wheat-free, gluten-free and Coeliac settings. It also lets you block ingredients or recipes that you never want suggested.

Understand the limits of a recipe filter

Recipe ingredients and representative nutrition data cannot establish the safety of a specific supermarket product. Check the actual ingredient list, allergen declarations and cross-contact statements every time you shop. Halal certification also depends on the particular product.

Important: Farefully cannot guarantee an allergen-free meal or suitable product. If a dietary restriction is medically necessary, follow advice from your healthcare professional and check every ingredient. Read our dietary safety guidance.

Plan without weakening your rules

Farefully filters unsuitable recipes before building a meal plan. If no recipes match every exclusion, it should tell you rather than silently relax the rules. Check custom recipes and exact product substitutions yourself.

Shop around your budget, too

Set your food budget and use pantry stock to reduce unnecessary shopping. Lower-cost options are only useful when they also meet the household's saved recipe restrictions.

Dietary filters are free

Essential dietary exclusions remain available in Farefully Free. Plus is intended for planning conveniences, not for charging people to protect dietary requirements.

Use your household size, preferences, pantry and budget. No payment details are needed to start.
