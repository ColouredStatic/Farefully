Dietary needs

Extra exclusions

Wheat-free is separate from gluten-free: it excludes wheat-based ingredients, while gluten-free / Coeliac also covers other gluten sources such as barley and rye. Packaged products still need label checking.

Allergens to filter

Important: Farefully filters recipes using known ingredient allergen data and makes configured substitutions where possible. It cannot verify a specific supermarket product, a “may contain” statement or manufacturing cross-contamination. For allergies or Coeliac disease, always check the exact pack label and allergen statement before buying or eating.

Privacy, safety & service information

Your household is saved to your Farefully account.
Your household setup, dietary preferences, pantry, meal plans, ratings and custom recipes are securely stored in our UK-hosted Supabase database and cached on this device. Only your signed-in account can access your household records. Saving needs an internet connection; if a save fails, Farefully keeps a pending copy in this browser and shows a warning. Your earlier beta profiles stay untouched on this device. You can delete a household using the Profiles menu.

Dietary & allergy safety

Always check the exact product label.
Farefully's dietary, allergen, Coeliac, wheat-free and Halal filters are planning aids. Ingredients, recipes and manufacturing information can change, and Farefully cannot guarantee cross-contamination status or certification. It is not medical or dietary advice.

Prices & weekly offers

Basket totals are estimates. Verified product snapshots and promotions are used only while recent or valid; otherwise Farefully falls back to its benchmark pricing model. The retailer's checkout price is always the final price.
