Your meals, one tap away

Give Farefully a home on your phone.

Open your household meal plan and shopping list straight from your home screen. No app-store account or additional purchase needed. Your existing Farefully login and Plus membership work here too.

Open my meal planner

Choose Install, or follow the instructions for your phone below.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the upward arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  4. Open Farefully from your Home Screen and sign in.

On some iOS versions, choose Open as Web App when prompted.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap Install Farefully above if your browser offers it.
  3. Otherwise open the browser menu ⋮ and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Accept the prompt and launch Farefully from your apps.

Worth knowing: Farefully still needs an internet connection to sign in, sync your family plans and refresh supermarket information. Installing it doesn't grant offline meal planning or change your membership.
