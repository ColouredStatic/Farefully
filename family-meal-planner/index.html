Plan the meals that get forgotten

Breakfast, lunch and snacks matter to the shopping budget as well. Farefully includes planned ingredients for all four meal types. Turn on packed-lunch mode if sandwiches and wraps are more practical for your household.

Keep preferences separate from hard exclusions

Rate recipes you enjoyed, and tell Farefully which ingredients or recipes should never be suggested. Dietary exclusions are not relaxed just to fill a week. Always verify exact packaged product labels and cross-contact risks.

Make next week easier

Once your household preferences and pantry are saved, you can generate another plan without repeating the setup. Swap individual dinners when circumstances change. Finish or reset an active shop before replacing meals, so the list remains consistent.

Plan for a whole fortnight

Free includes seven-day meal planning. Farefully Plus enables fourteen-day plans and two-trip shopping tools for households that prefer to plan further ahead.
