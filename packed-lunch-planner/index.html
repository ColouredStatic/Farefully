Practical meal planning

Packed lunches that belong in the weekly budget.

Lunchboxes can quietly become a second shopping list. Plan the lunches alongside breakfast, dinners and snacks so you can see the whole picture.

Start planning freeCompare Free and Plus

Choose a manageable lunch rotation

Start with a few combinations your household likes, such as cheese sandwiches, suitable hummus and vegetable wraps, or chicken sandwiches. Rotate fillings and sides rather than buying a different set of ingredients every day. Adapt ideas to dietary needs and school policies.

Count all the ingredients

Include bread or wraps, fillings, fruit and any planned snacks. Check what is already at home. If you buy additional snacks or pay for school meals separately, reserve an extra allowance rather than counting the same food twice.

Use packed-lunch mode

Farefully can prefer sandwiches and wraps over hot lunches for the whole household. It is a household-wide preference, not a separate school timetable. Your dietary exclusions and recipe preferences still apply.

Check school policies and food safety

Always check the school's food rules, especially around allergens. Check actual ingredient labels and keep perishable food appropriately chilled until eaten. Farefully is a planning aid, not a guarantee that a particular product is suitable for an allergy or dietary requirement.

Make the list work for the rest of the week

Use pantry quantities, select suitable dinners and review predicted spare packs. The planner can suggest other recipes that use spare ingredients, without adding them to your current basket automatically.

Do I need Plus?

No. Packed-lunch mode and seven-day meal planning are included in Farefully Free.
