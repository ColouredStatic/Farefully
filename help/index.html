Common questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. Create a free account with your email to set up your household and save meal plans and preferences securely across devices. Seven-day meal planning remains free, and Plus subscriptions are available for fourteen-day planning and advanced basket tools. Sign in and visit your Farefully account to manage billing.

Does Farefully Plus prescribe weight-loss calories?

No. The adult nutrition beta shows approximate ingredient-based calorie values where data is complete. Plus members aged 18 or over can optionally enter their own daily comparison figure and see other family dinner ideas. Farefully does not calculate a personalised weight-loss allowance, set calorie targets for children, or reduce portions automatically. Adult comparison settings are not saved and are cleared when the app page closes or reloads. Speak to an appropriate healthcare professional if you need individual dietary advice.

Can I install Farefully on my phone?

Yes. Follow our phone installation guide to put Farefully on your Home Screen. On iPhone, use your browser’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, use the Install app option in a supported browser. Farefully is a web app, not an app-store download, and it still needs an internet connection for sign-in and syncing.

Why is my household missing on another device?

Sign in using the same Farefully account on both devices and check the cloud-save indicator. Earlier browser-only beta profiles are not copied into the new account. If saving fails, reconnect to the internet and retry.

Are supermarket prices exact?

No. Farefully clearly separates recent verified product snapshots from modelled supermarket estimates. The retailer's checkout is always the final price.

Why are there no weekly offers for my supermarket?

Farefully only shows an offer when it has data it can verify and considers current. It would rather show nothing than invent a saving.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. Open Household → My recipes. Your recipes can then be used by the planner, swaps, pantry and basket.

How do I reset my data?

Open Household profiles and use Reset or Delete for the relevant profile. Clearing site data in your browser removes local cached information on that device, but does not delete records saved to your Farefully cloud account. For an account data copy or deletion request, email support@farefully.co.uk.
