🌿 Early family testers wanted

Help make dinner time a little easier.

We're inviting our first 20 households to test Farefully with real dinners, real budgets and real preferences. It's free.

Volunteer as a tester →Try Farefully now

Volunteering opens an email with your request prepared. No account or payment needed to try Farefully.

Who we're looking for: UK families, couples and individuals who plan meals and can share honest feedback. No technical expertise needed.

What you'll do

1

Set up your household

Choose the people you cook for, foods they enjoy, dietary needs, a budget and a supermarket.

2

Plan a genuine week

Build seven days of dinners, swap unsuitable meals and check the shopping list against your real cupboard.

3

Tell us how it went

A short email is enough. Tell us what saved time, what was confusing and whether you would use Farefully again.

What you get

Free access to the public beta and a chance to influence how Farefully develops. No purchase, prize or obligation to keep testing.

Your privacy and safety

During beta, your household profile stays in your browser. Please do not email sensitive family or medical information. Always check food labels, allergens and cross-contamination yourself, especially for Coeliac needs or allergies.
