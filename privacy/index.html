Privacy

Your household data, securely linked to your account.

How Farefully handles family meal-planning information during early access.

Short version: Farefully uses your verified email account to save household setup, dietary preferences, shopping lists and meal plans in a UK-hosted Supabase database. Data is also temporarily cached in your browser for resilience. Existing beta-only local profiles remain untouched, but are not automatically imported into your account.

What we collect and why

To provide the meal-planning service we process your account email, your household names and sizes, meal budgets, supermarket and ingredient preferences, dietary selections and allergies, pantry quantities, shopping-list progress, meal ratings, custom recipes and generated plans. You provide this information so Farefully can personalise recommendations and retrieve your household across devices.

Dietary and allergy choices can reveal sensitive health or religious information. Only enter details you are comfortable storing. Farefully uses these choices for meal filtering and planning, not advertising or profiling for unrelated purposes.

Optional adult nutrition comparisons

Farefully Plus includes an optional adult-only nutrition comparison and suggestions for other family dinners. If you enable this feature, the daily calorie reference you type is used only while that app page remains open. We do not put this number into your cloud-saved household record, and it is not retained after the page is reloaded or closed. You can leave it blank and use the recipe estimates without it.

Nutrition figures are approximations based on generic food-composition records, not personal dietary prescriptions. Suggestions use your existing household dietary preferences and do not create calorie goals for children. If you explicitly replace a dinner, that change to your family's shared meal plan is saved to your household account as usual.

Account and cloud storage

Farefully uses Supabase for account authentication and the household database, with the project hosted in the London, UK region. Your account signs in with email and password; passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored in the public website code. We restrict household data access so authenticated users can access only their own household records.

When you change a household, the planner attempts to save it to the cloud. Your browser also retains an account-specific local cache and pending changes if an internet connection fails. Changes made on two devices at the same time may conflict if two devices edit the same household before syncing.

Old beta data

If you used Farefully before account registration, earlier browser-only household data remains on the device until you clear it. The new account-based planner starts fresh instead of copying that data into your account. The old data will not be automatically uploaded.

Managing and deleting your information

You can delete individual household profiles from the planner. For account deletion, a copy of your data, correction or other UK data-protection requests, contact support@farefully.co.uk. We will verify the request before taking action. We retain information only as needed to provide the service, meet legitimate security and legal requirements, or handle your request.

Website hosting and technical information

Our public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs may be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers to deliver and protect the service. We use browser storage to maintain account sessions and keep unsent household changes.

Payments and gift memberships

Farefully Plus subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Stripe securely handles payment information, subscription renewal and customer billing separately from our meal-planning service. We already maintain protected account-level subscription and gift-access records in Supabase. We do not ask for payment card numbers on our Farefully account page.

Analytics and feedback emails

The planner does not currently use advertising trackers. If you separately subscribe to Farefully updates, MailerLite may process your email for invitations and feedback. That subscription is voluntary and can be cancelled independently of a Farefully account.

Contact

Questions about your data and privacy can be sent to support@farefully.co.uk.
