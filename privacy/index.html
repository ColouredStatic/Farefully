Privacy

Your household data stays local during beta.

Farefully is deliberately simple while we validate the product.

Short version: the Farefully app currently stores your household profile, food preferences, pantry, ratings and custom recipes in your browser's local storage. Farefully does not currently create a user account or upload that profile to a Farefully database.

What the app stores

On this device Farefully may store household names, household size, budget, supermarket choice, food preferences, dietary selections, pantry quantities, meal ratings, recent plans and custom recipes.

Where it is stored

This information is stored in the browser used to access Farefully. It is not automatically synchronised between devices. Clearing site data in your browser, or deleting a Farefully household profile, can remove the local copy.

Website hosting

The public website is hosted using third-party infrastructure. Ordinary technical data such as IP address, browser information and request logs may be processed by hosting and domain providers as needed to deliver the site securely.

Email

If you email Farefully, the information in that message is processed so we can answer your enquiry or investigate a beta issue.

Analytics

Farefully does not currently use advertising trackers in the planner. If privacy-friendly analytics are introduced later, this notice will be updated before they are relied on.

Optional beta-tester emails

If people explicitly opt in to beta-testing updates, their email addresses may be handled through MailerLite for invitations and feedback requests. This is separate from the locally stored household profile. Optional emails can be unsubscribed from at any time; contacting support alone does not automatically join a mailing list.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@farefully.co.uk.
