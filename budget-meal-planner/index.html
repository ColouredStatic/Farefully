Practical meal planning

Make your weekly food budget work harder.

A useful meal plan starts with what you can actually spend, not a list of recipes that looks lovely until you reach the checkout.

Start planning freeCompare Free and Plus

Start with the money available

Set a realistic limit for this shopping trip. If the same budget needs to cover toiletries, cleaning products or extra lunchbox food, reserve that money before choosing meals. Avoid counting breakfast, lunch and snacks twice: Farefully already includes planned meals in its grocery basket.

Example: With a £90 whole-shop budget and £15 reserved for non-plan purchases, you have £75 for planned groceries. This is an example of budget allocation, not a claim that a particular family can eat for £75.

Check the cupboards before shopping

Look for rice, pasta, tins, freezer portions and vegetables that need using. Enter quantities you actually have. Farefully deducts pantry stock before rounding up the packs you need to buy.

Build a flexible week, not seven unrelated recipes

Choose meals that can share ingredients. A pack of carrots might go into a cottage pie, a vegetable soup and a side dish. A bag of rice can support several dinners. Check use-by dates and storage instructions before saving food for later.

When the budget is tighter than usual

Budget Rescue previews lower-cost dinner swaps without overwriting your plan. Payday Mode lets you plan for a shorter period with the money left until you are paid. The cupboard meal finder looks for suitable recipes using your actual saved stock.

Track the actual checkout

Enter prices from your own shop or save the final receipt total. Farefully distinguishes recent public price observations, personal pack prices and benchmark estimates. Prices and stock vary by shop; a basket estimate is not a guaranteed checkout price.

Does it cost anything to start?

No. Seven-day planning, budget controls, pantry deductions, dietary exclusions and the standard shopping list are free. Plus adds fourteen-day planning and advanced shopping conveniences.
